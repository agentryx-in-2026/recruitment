HireStream Lite · Phase 1 · Demo for the Secretary

Demo script: one government job, from the Ministry batch to a worker placed abroad

A 26-minute live demonstration of hirestream-lite.agentryx.dev for the Secretary and HPSEDC. It follows the order HPSEDC will work in: set the rules, publish the vacancies, applicants register, HPSEDC interviews and selects, documents and departure, then aftercare and oversight. Prepared 15 November 2026 by Agentryx; every step below was checked on the live build the same day.

26 min9 scenes, plus 5–10 min questions
3 screensAdmin, Placement Cell, applicant on a phone
1 storyGulf welder and caregiver vacancies, Himachal applicants
Hindishown on the applicant screens

Who does what. In this phase, HPSEDC itself acts as the recruiting agency: its Overseas Placement Cell publishes the jobs, registers and screens applicants, arranges interviews, collects documents and handles departure and welfare. No private agency stands between the applicant and the Government.

The message in one sentence. HPSEDC runs overseas placement end to end from one portal: the Ministry batch goes in, HPSEDC publishes it, Himachal applicants register on a phone in Hindi, HPSEDC interviews and selects, tracks every document and the visa until departure, and keeps watch on the worker for 90 days after they land.

Before the demo

The day before

  • Decide email confirmation: there is no email provider yet (HL-003). Either connect one, or switch off Confirm email before applying in Admin → System Config so the new applicant in scene 3 can apply.
  • Confirm all vacancies are open with a deadline (Placement Cell → My Jobs).
  • Rehearse once end to end; then reset the rehearsal records (interview, selection) or rehearse on the test copy.
  • Phone: install nothing — the portal runs in the phone's browser. Set the phone to Hindi keyboard support.
  • Download the three walkthrough videos to the laptop as the fallback (Verify → Guides & videos).

30 minutes before

  • Open hirestream-lite.agentryx.dev in the three windows and sign in.
  • Zoom the laptop browser to 125% so the room can read it.
  • Check the sign-in page shows no "Demo Mode" button.
  • Have the Ministry batch file on the laptop: Ministry_job_batch_demo.csv (in the Documents library, next to this script). It includes one deliberately bad row, so the importer can show a rejection.
  • Mute notifications on the laptop and phone.

Likely questions

QuestionAnswer
Is applicants' data safe?Passwords are stored hashed, sessions end after 30 minutes idle, each role sees only its own screens, Aadhaar is masked, and every action is in the audit log. A security audit (OWASP) has been done.
Can it take a big batch — thousands of applicants?Yes; sizing for 5,000 users at once is part of the production server request.
What about applicants who cannot use a phone?They can leave their name and number with Request a callback; the requests appear in the Admin Console for HPSEDC to call back.
Is HPSEDC an agency now?For this phase, yes — HPSEDC itself acts as the government recruiting agency, like ODEPC in Kerala or TOMCOM in Telangana. Applicants deal only with HPSEDC; there is no private middleman.
Can private agencies and foreign employers use it?The portal already supports them; they are switched off for phase 1 and can be switched on later, working under HPSEDC's oversight.
Will there be an app?Yes, Android first, working on the same data; it follows the web launch.
Can applicants get SMS or email updates?In-app notifications and email work today (verification codes, password reset, status emails). SMS needs HPSEDC's SMS gateway account.

If something goes wrong

  • The internet drops: play the walkthrough video for the same scene (Applicant, Placement Cell or Admin; each has chapters).
  • A page is slow: keep talking to the scene's message; never refresh repeatedly.
  • A sign-in is refused: use another prepared applicant at the same stage (Sanjay Chauhan, Suresh Thakur or Anita Kumari).
  • Do not press Run lifecycle in the Admin Console during the demo, and do not change settings you have not rehearsed.
