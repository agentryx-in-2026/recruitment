About this guide

The Admin Console is the oversight and configuration centre of HireStream. HPSEDC administrators use it to load government job batches, decide what the application form asks, define the eligibility rules for each trade, watch compliance and welfare across all candidates, handle complaints and fraud reports, and change how the portal behaves — all without a software release.

Day-to-day recruitment work (posting single jobs, interviews, selection, appointment letters, welfare check-ins) is done by the Placement Cell with the HPSEDC agency account — see the HPSEDC as Super Agency User Guide.

Signing in and the console

Sign in on the HireStream sign-in page with your administrator username or email and password. The Admin Console opens.

  • Header buttons — one-click downloads: Candidates CSV, Jobs CSV, Applications CSV, Placements CSV.
  • Left menu, in groups:
    • Overview & Analytics — Overview, Reports, Funnel
    • People & Orgs — Users, Callbacks, Placement Support
    • Operations — Import Jobs, Lifecycle, Drives, Matching Engine, Professions, Form Fields, Deployment Readiness, Welfare SLA
    • Risk & Compliance — Compliance, Audit Log, Fraud Watch, Duplicates
    • Communication — Messages, Grievances, Notifications
    • System — Architecture, Integrations, Countries, System Config
Agencies, Employers and Leaderboard menu items are hidden while agency and employer self-registration are switched off (single-agency mode). They reappear if those capabilities are enabled in System Config.

Security: sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity. Change your password from your name (top right) → Change Password. Never share administrator credentials.

Overview and analytics

Overview

Tiles for registered candidates, open job vacancies and placements; the Application Pipeline (registered, applied, placed) with a chart by stage; Candidates by District; Jobs by Destination Country; Pending Verifications (drive approvals, open grievances); top skills in demand; and recent activity.

Reports

Six ready-made reports, each with a CSV download: by district, by country, by placement status, by agency, by skill, and by sector / company.

Funnel

Shows how many candidates reach each stage and the drop-off from the previous stage, optionally filtered by country.

Importing government job batches

Government vacancies usually arrive as an Excel or CSV sheet. The Smart Job Importer (Operations → Import Jobs) turns the sheet into live jobs in a few clicks.

  1. Click Choose file and select the .csv or .xlsx file (maximum 5 MB, 2,000 rows).
  2. HireStream reads the column headings and maps them automatically to job fields — Job title, Company / Employer, Country, Location, Category, Salary, Vacancies, Last date, Experience, Qualification, Description, Requirements, Skills. Correct any mapping with the drop-downs; choose — Ignore — for columns you do not need.
  3. Every row is checked and marked Valid (green), Warning (amber — will be imported, but read the note, e.g. unknown category left blank, or a similar job already posted) or Error (red — will be skipped, e.g. country missing).
  4. Click Import N jobs. Valid and warning rows become live jobs owned by the HPSEDC Placement Cell. Error rows are skipped.
  5. The import is listed under Recent imports (file name, jobs posted, rows skipped).
Preparing the sheet.Use one row per vacancy with clear headings, e.g. Job Title, Country, Company, Location, Category, Monthly Salary, Vacancies, Last Date, Job Description, Experience. Use the portal's category names (e.g. Construction Worker, Healthcare, Driver, Electrician, Hospitality Staff) so that trade documents are requested correctly. Dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Nothing is saved until you click Import.

Professions and the application form

Professions & eligibility

Operations → Professions defines the trades candidates choose from, and what each trade must hold. Changes are live for candidates immediately.

Click Edit on a profession (or Add a profession) to change:

  • Name (English) and Name (Hindi), and the order in the list.
  • Job categories — which job categories this trade answers. A selected candidate is asked for this trade's documents only for jobs in these categories.
  • Accepted qualifications — any one of them satisfies the trade.
  • Ask "what specifically can you do?" and the list of specific skills.
  • Required documents and Optional documents — picked from the portal's document list (documents marked after selection, such as PCC and medical, are always asked later).
  • What the candidate is told (English and Hindi) — shown the moment they pick the trade.
  • The key of a profession cannot be changed after creation (candidates' records point to it).
  • The extra questions of Nurse, Caregiver, Driver, Electrician, Welder are part of the portal itself and cannot be edited here.
  • The eye icon hides a profession from new candidates. Nothing is deleted — candidates who already chose it keep it. Click the eye again to show it.

Form Fields

Operations → Form Fields decides what the application form asks, page by page.

  • Shown / Hidden — whether the field appears on the form.
  • Must fill / Optional — whether it is compulsory. A hidden field is never compulsory.
  • A padlock means the field cannot be switched off (e.g. passport expiry), because the portal depends on it.
  • Full name, date of birth, gender, mobile, email and permanent address are always asked and are not listed.
  • Two rows are options rather than fields: "I do not have a passport yet" (on by default) and the "I am not sure" choice for ECR status (off by default).

Changes apply to candidates immediately.

People

Users

People & Orgs → Users lists all accounts with tabs Candidates, Agencies, Employers, Staff, All, showing username, email, role, whether active, and last login. Click Disable to block an account from signing in, or Enable to restore it.

Take care when disabling accounts.Do not disable your own account, the HPSEDC agency account or a super administrator. Creating staff accounts and changing roles is done by the super administrator.

Callbacks

Candidates who asked HPSEDC to phone them (when the assisted-callback option is switched on). Call the candidate, then mark the request done.

Placement Support

Monthly check-ins ("I'm fine" / "I need help") and problems reported by workers already overseas — salary not paid, contract differences, safety or harassment, health, passport held. Follow up and update the status of each.

Deployment, compliance and welfare

Compliance

Risk & Compliance → Compliance tracks Emigration Act / MEA compliance across all candidates:

  • Coverage against targets — passport on file, ECR/ECNR declared, PCC submitted, medical fit, PDO completed, PBBY enrolled, IELTS recorded.
  • Visa pipeline — deployed workers by visa status (not applied, applied, approved, rejected).
  • Risk flags — placed without PDO, placed without PBBY, placed without passport on file, passport expiring in under 6 months, placed with visa not yet approved, visa rejected.

Deployment Readiness

Operations → Deployment Readiness: how many selected candidates are travel-ready, in compliance or blocked, by stage, with Export CSV for follow-up.

Welfare SLA

Operations → Welfare SLA lists every overdue 30/60/90-day welfare check-in (candidate, placement, country, milestone, days overdue), and placements without a start date. Ask the Placement Cell to record the check-ins. "All welfare check-ins up to date" means nothing is overdue.

Audit, fraud and duplicates

Audit Log

The latest 200 recorded actions — status changes, settings changes, compliance and visa updates, profile changes — with when, who, action, resource and details (including from → to). Filter by resource or by action text. Click Refresh for the newest entries.

Fraud Watch

Recent fraud reports from candidates ("someone asked me for money") and any agency with a spike of grievances in the last 30 days. Treat every fraud report as urgent.

Duplicates

Groups of candidate records that share an Aadhaar number, phone number or email. Review each group and merge duplicates so a person has one record.

Messages, grievances and notifications

Messages

The shared Ask HPSEDC inbox. Administrators can read and answer candidates' messages in the same way as the Placement Cell; the unread count shows on the menu.

Grievances

Opens the Grievances page with the All grievances tab (administrators only) in addition to My grievances and Assigned to me. Fraud reports are routed to the administrators' queue.

Submitted→Start Review→Under review→Mark Action Taken→Complainant confirms → Resolved

When marking Action Taken, describe what you did — the complainant sees it and confirms whether the problem is resolved, or reopens it. Use Internal Note for staff-only notes. Service levels per category (default 2 days for fraud, 3 for application and technical issues, 7 otherwise) and automatic escalation are set in System Config.

Notifications

Communication → Notifications lists the message templates sent for each event to each role (candidate, agency, admin). Click Edit template to change the title and text, switch a template off, or set it to hide the employer's company name in negative messages.

The super administrator signs in to a separate Super Admin console. Super administrators cannot use demo quick-login. Every super-administrator change is recorded in the audit log.

Go-live checklist

  1. Feature flag "Quick login" OFF (Super Admin → Feature Flags). Confirm the sign-in page shows no demo logins.
  2. Email gateway configured and tested (Integrations) — or switch off "Require a confirmed email before applying".
  3. Helpline number and hours filled in (System Config → contact).
  4. Passwords: super administrator, administrators and the hpsedc_agency account changed from any test values; demo accounts removed.
  5. System Controls lockdown bypass key changed from its default.
  6. Terminal statuses: consider locking placed.
  7. Jobs: active, with deadlines, so the nightly lifecycle does not close them unexpectedly.
  8. Professions and Form Fields reviewed and agreed by HPSEDC.
  9. Notification templates reviewed (English and Hindi text).
  10. Backups scheduled and a test restore done.
