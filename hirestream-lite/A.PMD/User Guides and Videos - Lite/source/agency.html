About this guide

In this release of HireStream, HPSEDC acts as the single super agency. Private recruitment agencies and employer self-registration are switched off. The HPSEDC Overseas Placement Cell receives government job batches, publishes the vacancies, interviews every applicant, selects candidates, and manages their departure and welfare.

This guide is for Placement Cell staff who do that work every day. Administrator tasks — importing job batches from Excel, configuring the form and professions, system settings and portal-wide oversight — are covered in the Admin User Guide.

The recruitment pipeline

The portal runs the Direct, four-stage pipeline:

Submitted→Interview→Selected→Placed·RejectedWithdrawn
StageWho moves itWhat happens
SubmittedCandidate appliesThe application appears in your pipeline. No documents are requested.
InterviewYou — Schedule interviewThe candidate is notified and can confirm, ask to reschedule, or decline.
SelectedYou — Record outcome → Pass / SelectAn offer is created automatically (valid 7 days), the candidate's details are snapshotted, and the candidate is told which documents to upload.
PlacedCandidate — Accept OfferAllowed only after you upload the signed appointment letter. Welfare check-ins start.
RejectedYou — Reject or Fail / RejectThe candidate is notified; the employer's name is hidden in negative messages.
Documents are requested only at selection.Around 20,000 people may apply; only the few hundred selected are asked for police clearance, medical, passport, ID, education, experience, trade and country documents. Never ask an applicant for documents before selection.

Signing in

Sign in on the HireStream sign-in page with the Placement Cell account hpsedc_agency (email agency@hpsedc.gov.in) and its password.

Use the HPSEDC agency account.Every vacancy in single-agency mode belongs to this account. Staff signed in with any other agency account will see an empty dashboard and cannot act on applications. Do not share the password outside the Placement Cell, and change it regularly (your name at top right → Change Password).

Sessions end after 30 minutes without activity. Sign out on shared computers.

  • Agency card (top left) — "HPSEDC — Overseas Placement Cell", the licence number, the Verified Agency badge and the Post Job button.
  • Left menu — Dashboard · My Jobs · Applications Pipeline · Open Requisitions · Candidates · Drives · Reports · Activity · Messages (with an unread count).

Posting and managing jobs

Post a new job

  1. Click Post Job. The Post a New Job form opens.
  2. Role basics — Job title, Employer / company (the overseas employer), Country, City (or Other city…), and Job category. The category decides which trade documents a selected candidate must upload and how the job is grouped and matched — always set it.
  3. Compensation & experience — choose the currency, period (per month) and the fixed salary amount; add an optional note (e.g. "tax-free, accommodation included"); choose the minimum experience.
  4. Hiring criteria (optional) — minimum qualification and required languages (CEFR levels).
  5. Description & skills — the job description (mention accommodation, visa support and benefits) and the required skills (type a skill and press Enter or click Add).
  6. Click Publish Job. The job is live immediately. Save as Draft keeps it hidden until you publish it.
Large government batches (tens of vacancies from an Excel sheet) are loaded by an administrator through Import Jobs — see the Admin User Guide. Imported jobs appear in your My Jobs list, owned by the Placement Cell, and can also carry a vacancy count and last date.

My Jobs

My Jobs lists every vacancy. Filter by All / Active / Drafts / Closed, search by title, company, location or skill, filter by country or company, and sort by newest, oldest, deadline or priority. Each card shows the number of applicants. Icons on a card let you edit, clone as a new draft, or delete (only when it has no applicants).

The Applications Pipeline

Click Applications Pipeline in the menu (or a stage on the dashboard). It shows every application across all jobs on one page. By default it opens on Submitted — new applications waiting for an interview.

  • Stage tiles — All, Submitted, Interview, Selected, Placed, Rejected, Withdrawn, with counts. Click one to filter.
  • Search — candidate name, job title, company or skill.
  • ⏳ Stale — only applications that have waited 7 days or more in their stage (🔥 for 14+ days). Use it daily so nobody is forgotten.
  • Sort — newest, oldest, best match, longest in stage. Filters — country, company, job.
  • Each row shows the candidate, match %, stage, days in stage, job and country, and the actions for that stage.

The candidate profile

Click View on any row to open the full candidate profile.

  • Contact details with email and Call buttons, skills, education and experience.
  • Your private tags — your own notes, e.g. "good-english", "passport-expires-aug". Visible only to the Placement Cell account.
  • Visa / country rejections — record countries that refused this candidate a visa; jobs in those countries are hidden from them.
  • Documents — every uploaded document with its type, a Verified badge when checked, and Preview / download.
  • Deployment Readiness, Pre-Departure Compliance, and — once selected — Visa / passport assistance, Appointment & travel and welfare check-ins (see the following sections).

Interviews

Schedule an interview

  1. In the pipeline (Submitted), click Schedule interview on the application.
  2. Choose the Date and Time, and the Mode — In-person or Virtual.
  3. For in-person, enter the location (e.g. "HPSEDC Office, Nigam Vihar, Shimla"). For virtual, a Meeting link is required.
  4. Enter the Interviewer name (shown to the candidate) and, if the interview is part of a recruitment drive, choose the Drive.
  5. Click Schedule & Notify Candidate. The application moves to Interview and the candidate is notified.

In the Interview stage each row shows whether the candidate has Confirmed or Declined. A reminder is sent to the candidate before the interview. Interviews can be added to your calendar (.ics).

Respond to a reschedule request

If a candidate asks to move the interview, an orange Respond to reschedule button appears. Click it to see the current time, the candidate's reason and the time they proposed, then choose:

  • Accept proposed time
  • Set a different time — pick a new date and time, then Set new time & notify
  • Keep original time (decline request)

The candidate is notified of your decision.

Record the interview outcome

  1. Click Outcome on the application.
  2. Choose Pass / Select or Fail / Reject.
  3. Optionally give a rating (1 Poor – 5 Excellent). Add notes — notes are required when rejecting.
  4. Click Select Candidate (or Reject Candidate).

On Select, HireStream automatically: moves the application to Selected; creates the offer (valid 7 days); snapshots the candidate's details as they were at selection; and notifies the candidate of the documents now required.

Reject without an interview

Click Reject on any row. You can type optional feedback for the candidate. The candidate is told the application did not go ahead; the employer's name is not shown.

After selection: pre-departure

Open a selected candidate from the pipeline (Selected tile → View).

Deployment Readiness

Lists what is done and what is still missing: offer accepted, passport, police clearance, medical, visa, pre-departure orientation (PDO), PBBY insurance, emigration clearance, appointment letter and travel.

Pre-Departure Compliance (MEA / Emigration Act)

Record the confirmed results: ECR status, Police Clearance (Submitted / Pending / Not required), Medical Fitness (Fit / Pending / Unfit), IELTS band, PDO completed with date, and PBBY insurance (Enrolled / Pending / Not required) with policy number. Passport number and expiry can be corrected here; the Aadhaar number is shown masked and cannot be edited. Click Save compliance. Every change is written to the audit log.

ECR candidates.For ECR passport holders travelling to ECR countries, PDO and PBBY must be completed before the travel date.

Visa / passport assistance

Choose the status — Not applied yet, Application submitted, Approved, Rejected — add an optional note to the candidate (e.g. "Embassy appointment on 12 June") and click Update & notify. Every change is kept in the History list and the candidate is notified.

Appointment & travel

  • Travel / start date — choose the date and click the save icon. For an ECR candidate the date is refused ("Couldn't save start date") until PDO and PBBY have been recorded in Pre-Departure Compliance — record those first.
  • Appointment letter — click Upload signed file (PDF, JPG or PNG), or paste an external link and save. View current opens the uploaded letter; Replace file uploads a new one.
The candidate cannot accept the offer until the appointment letter is uploaded.Upload it as soon as the employer signs it — the offer expires after 7 days. When the candidate accepts, the application moves to Placed automatically. Staff cannot set "Placed" by hand.

After placement: welfare

For every placed worker there are 30-, 60- and 90-day welfare check-ins. Open the candidate (Placed tile → View) and, under Post-placement welfare check-ins:

  1. Click Record on the due check-in.
  2. Choose OK, Concerns, No response or Not applicable, and add a short note.
  3. Click Save.

Workers are also prompted automatically at 30/60/90 days and can report problems themselves. Overdue check-ins appear on the administrators' Welfare SLA monitor. Record Concerns honestly — they drive follow-up.

Candidates and recruitment drives

Candidate pool

Candidates in the menu lists all registered candidates. Search by name, email or skill; filter by experience (Junior / Mid / Senior), profile complete or incomplete, and candidates open to outreach. Click View for a quick profile with email and call buttons.

Recruitment drives

  1. Open Drives and click Create Drive.
  2. Enter the title, description, date, location, target roles and expected number of candidates.
  3. Submit. The drive goes to an administrator for approval.
  4. Once approved, open the drive to Notify candidates, invite registrants, mark attendance, schedule interviews, and record results (Selected / Hold / Rejected) with feedback.
Selecting through a drive.A drive result of "Selected" records the decision but does not create the offer. To start the offer and document process, also record the outcome from the Applications Pipeline (Outcome → Pass / Select).

Open Requisitions

This screen is for employer requisitions in the marketplace mode. While employer registration is switched off, it shows "No open requisitions".

Messages and grievances

Messages — Ask HPSEDC

Candidates' questions arrive under Messages; the red number shows unread threads. Click a candidate's thread, type your reply in Type your reply… and click the send button. The candidate sees the reply on their Help page. Reply within working hours; keep answers short and in simple language (Hindi where helpful).

Grievances

Formal complaints are separate from messages. Open Grievances at the top of the page. Assigned to me lists grievances routed to the Placement Cell — complaints about an application are routed here automatically.

ActionWhen
Start ReviewYou have begun looking into it.
Mark Action TakenYou have acted; describe what you did — the complainant sees this and confirms whether it is resolved.
Internal NoteA note visible only to staff.

Fraud reports ("someone asked me for money") go to the administrators' queue. Grievances not handled within the service level (default 3 days for application issues) are escalated.

Reports

Reports in the menu gives the Placement Cell its own management view:

  • Headline — active jobs, applicants, accepted placements, conversion rate.
  • Pipeline health — where candidates drop out, stale applicants, time to placement, this month vs last.
  • Operations & SLA — welfare check-ins due at 30/60/90 days, compliance alerts, grievances.
  • Strategic insights — top employers, skill gap (demand vs supply), time in stage.
  • HPSEDC compliance (Emigration Act 1983) — PBBY and PDO coverage, ECR / non-ECR mix, top destination countries, applications over time.

For per-job applicant lists use Export CSV on the job page. Portal-wide CSV exports (candidates, jobs, applications, placements) are in the Admin Console.

Daily checklist and quick reference

Every working day

  1. Check Messages and reply to new questions.
  2. Pipeline → Submitted → schedule interviews; use ⏳ Stale to catch anything waiting 7+ days.
  3. Pipeline → Interview → respond to reschedule requests; record outcomes for interviews held.
  4. Pipeline → Selected → upload appointment letters, update visa status and compliance; chase missing documents.
  5. Pipeline → Placed → record due welfare check-ins.
  6. Grievances → Assigned to me.

Current limitations

The following points apply to this release. Please work around them as described until they are addressed.

LimitationWork-around
The dashboard Applicant Pipeline, the job page stage tiles and the Reports funnel still show six stages (including Reviewed and Shortlisted), which are always zero in the four-stage pipeline.Ignore the Reviewed and Shortlisted columns. The Applications Pipeline page shows the correct four stages.
A greyed-out Reviewed button appears on submitted applications; the board view's "Change status" menu offers Reviewed / Shortlisted, which the portal refuses.Use Schedule interview, Outcome and Reject only.
Documents can be previewed but there is no on-screen button to mark a candidate's document as verified.Record compliance results in Pre-Departure Compliance; ask the administrator about document verification.
There is no bulk "select" button for many candidates at once.Record outcomes one by one.
The Post Job form does not ask for the number of vacancies or the last date.Use the administrator's Import Jobs for batches that need a vacancy count and deadline.
