HireStream Lite · UAT preparation

FRS coverage and the phase-1 demo workflow

How the contracted FRS (Overseas Placement Portal & Mobile Application, HPSEDC) is met by HireStream Lite v1.1.0, what we add on top to make phase 1 practical, the end-to-end story to show the Secretary, and what to fix before UAT. Checked against the live code and the Lite Verify project, 14 November 2026.

Summary

Phase 1 is an HPSEDC-run operation. Government job batches arrive through the Ministry (for example a few hundred Gulf vacancies), HPSEDC publishes them, applicants register and apply, and HPSEDC interviews, selects and sends workers abroad. Private agencies and overseas employers are dormant in Lite: built, but switched off.

Of 50 FRS line items, 34 are met on screen today, 4 are built but dormant by HPSEDC’s phase-1 choice, and the rest need either credentials from HPSEDC or a small amount of work. Only two are real functional gaps.

The three roles in Lite

Applicant

Self-registers, applies, attends interview, uploads documents only once selected, accepts the offer. Mostly blue-collar, on phones, in Hindi.

HPSEDC Placement Cell

One login, hpsedc_agency, acting as the government recruiting agency. Two jobs in the demo: publish vacancies, then match, interview and select candidates through to departure.

HPSEDC Admin

Imports Ministry batches, sets what the form asks and what each trade must hold, switches optional functions on or off, oversees compliance, complaints and reports.

Note on “HPSEDC publishes the batch”. Single jobs are posted by the Placement Cell. Bulk Ministry batches from Excel are loaded through Admin → Import Jobs, and the imported jobs belong to the Placement Cell. In the demo, show the import as the Admin, then the Placement Cell working those jobs.

Making it easy for blue-collar applicants

HPSEDC’s final feedback was to make registration very easy. Lite offers these routes today:

1. Self-registration, simplified

One four-page form, about five minutes. Hindi throughout, voice input on text fields, nothing to upload, answers saved on every page, printable A4 copy. Live

2. “HPSEDC will call me”

Applicant leaves name, mobile and skills; HPSEDC calls back and fills the form for them. Built, but switched off on Lite (capability.assisted_callback_enabled). Turn on to show

3. Documents by phone photo

Certificates are asked only after selection, and can be photographed with the phone camera. The few hundred selected, not the thousands who apply, carry this cost. Live

4. Mobile app

Android app (Expo) for candidates. Currently built against the old staging server, not Lite. Must be re-pointed and retested before it is shown. Not ready

A CV/resume parser also exists on the server but has no screen in Lite; keep it out of the phase-1 demo.

Fix before UAT

Must fix (demo breaks without them)

  1. All vacancies are closed: reopen with deadlines (HL-005)
  2. No email delivery: configure a mail provider, or switch off email confirmation (HL-003)
  3. First Submit bounces back to page 1 (HL-001)
  4. Demo-login button on the sign-in page; turn quick login off (HL-004)
  5. Re-point the mobile app to Lite and rebuild, or leave it out of the demo
  6. Make the server IP static so DNS cannot break again

Should fix (closes FRS gaps)

  1. Add country preference to the form (C5)
  2. Document Verify / Reject for staff (G5, HL-008)
  3. Forgot-password page (HL-002)
  4. Applicant progress bar off by one stage (HL-006)
  5. Agency funnel and reports still show six stages (HL-010)
  6. FAQ / announcement editor for admins (S9), if time allows

Needs HPSEDC

  • HIM Access SSO client credentials (C2)
  • UIDAI / Aadhaar API access (C1)
  • DigiLocker API access (O3)
  • SMS gateway account, e.g. NIC SMS or MSG91 (O4)
  • Production hosting size for 5,000 concurrent users (O10)
